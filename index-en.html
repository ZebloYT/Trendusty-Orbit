// How It Works

From raw signals to a clear picture

Three simple stages transform noisy global radio telemetry and public sensor APIs into unified operational intelligence.

01

Connect

Over 20 live feeds stream in continuously — ADS-B radio receivers, AIS maritime vessels, satellite TLEs, USGS seismic networks, NASA FIRMS fire satellites, and cyber threat nodes.

02

Fuse

Every entity is normalized and plotted onto one interactive 3D globe. Layers stack cleanly: flights over ships over active fault lines over conflict zones — filtered exactly the way you need.

03

Act

Investigate with the built-in OSINT toolkit, open instant live street camera streams, lock camera tracking onto moving targets, and export tactical intelligence packages to your team.
